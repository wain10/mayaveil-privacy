MayaVeil privacy policy

Schopenhauer’s Lab operates no MayaVeil backend. The app does not send us your browsing history, searches, page contents, files, passwords or settings. We do not track how you use the app. MayaVeil processes pages and files on your device. A copy you choose to save or share can remain after the session.

Who develops MayaVeil?

MayaVeil is an Android app developed by Schopenhauer’s Lab. For privacy questions or requests, email schopenhauerslab [at] proton [dot] me.

What happens when I browse?

You can use MayaVeil without an account. The app handles web addresses, search terms, page contents, cookies and information you enter on websites so it can load pages and keep you signed in during the session. MayaVeil has no advertising or analytics service of its own.

When you open a page, the website and any services it loads can receive your IP address and the information you send them. Searches go to the search provider you select. Your network and DNS providers can see connection information. MayaVeil Basic connects directly, so websites can see your IP address.

Can websites use my camera or location?

MayaVeil Basic declines requests for your location, camera, microphone and website notifications. It can play audio or video during the session.

Who helps check whether a site is safe?

MayaVeil uses Mozilla’s GeckoView browser engine with Google Safe Browsing. The engine may contact Google and Mozilla to update threat lists or security settings and to check suspected threats. Those requests can reveal your IP address and information used to check a site. Certificate checks may also contact the authority that issued a site’s certificate. These services apply their own retention policies. MayaVeil disables real-time Safe Browsing lookups and configures the engine to disable telemetry uploads and its crash reporter.

What happens to files I open, save or share?

Files you open from your device stay where they were. MayaVeil receives read access to the selected file. It displays supported PDFs, images, text, spreadsheets in CSV format, JSON, audio and video from working memory. An Android version that supports protected PDFs can use a password you enter to display the pages locally. Closing a separately opened file clears the reader’s working copy and returns to MayaVeil home.

If you share a link, the app you choose receives the source address. If you share a file, that app can read and keep a copy. If you save a file, a copy stays in the location you choose until you delete it. For these file transfers, MayaVeil holds a separate copy in RAM. Access expires after ten minutes, or earlier when a save finishes or is canceled.

What happens when I archive a page?

If you choose Archive this page, MayaVeil creates a read-only snapshot of the printable page. It can contain personal details visible on that page, including content you viewed while signed in. MayaVeil compresses the snapshot on your device in the archive format you choose. You choose where to save it or which app receives it.

You can also choose Archive a file to compress an original document of any format, up to 32 MiB. Its filename and contents are inside the archive. Unlocking it lets you save the original or preview a supported format. Archiving a file does not run its contents or change the source file.

Standard ZIP creates an unencrypted .zip on your device. Anyone with the archive can see its filenames and extract the contents with an ordinary ZIP tool, without MayaVeil or a password.

With Password, the file is encrypted on the device. Anyone who obtains both the file and the correct password can read it with a compatible reader. We receive neither and have no recovery key. With Public copy, anyone who receives the file can open it and forward it without a password. When you open either kind, MayaVeil reads the page in memory. Ending the session leaves previously saved and shared archives in place.

Plain links reveal the address to the app or person you share them with. A protected link stores only the encrypted address, either in the part of an HTTPS link after the # sign or in a .maya file. MayaVeil encrypts and unlocks it on your device. Opening the HTTPS link in another browser can contact our static site’s host, which receives ordinary connection information. The browser does not send the fragment in that request. Enter the password in MayaVeil. Once unlocked, the app connects to the destination as it would during normal browsing.

What does session cleanup erase?

Switching apps, locking the phone, ending the session yourself or reaching your chosen inactivity timeout ends the session. Opening Android’s share or save screen also ends browsing. The release app asks Android to block screenshots and hide the current page in the recent-apps preview.

When you leave the app or end the session, MayaVeil closes the session and attempts to overwrite the writable file memory it controls before releasing it. The browser engine can also write temporary files to your device. MayaVeil removes session files during cleanup and retries at startup if cleanup was interrupted.

Android, your keyboard, websites and apps you share with may keep their own copies. Closing MayaVeil cannot erase those copies or a file you saved. To delete information you sent to a website, contact that website.

Which settings survive a session?

Your search provider, appearance, navigation bar position and optional session timeout are encrypted on your device and excluded from Android backup. To delete these preferences and their encryption key, open Settings and choose Forget saved settings. You can also clear MayaVeil’s storage in Android settings.

Contact and this policy page

Email you choose to send is separate from using the app. Our mailbox is hosted by Proton AG in Switzerland. We use your address and what you send to reply to enquiries and handle privacy requests. For ordinary correspondence our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you. When the GDPR requires us to act on a rights request, our basis is that legal obligation. We delete your email correspondence when your request is resolved. Please leave out passwords, browsing history and private files.

You can ask for access, correction, erasure or restriction of personal data we hold and object to processing based on legitimate interests. We normally respond within one month. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or your local supervisory authority. We have no backend copy of your sessions to retrieve or erase.
